# Upstream Projects

## Anthropic K12 Teacher Skills

https://github.com/anthropics/k12-teacher-skills

Primary upstream for pedagogical workflow ideas such as lesson planning, lesson differentiation, lesson preparation, and small diagnostic Checks for Understanding.

This repository does **not** require Claude or the Learning Commons Knowledge Graph. Where upstream workflows assume US standards or connectors, this project replaces them with profile/local-reference logic and teacher-provided materials.

## China K12 Community Adaptation

https://github.com/flyboat403/k12-skills

Important reference for China curriculum localization, local-reference organization, information-technology routing, and Windows-aware usage. We do not blindly vendor the repository; rules are reviewed against official sources and current upstream behavior.

## Authoritative policy sources

Curriculum and regional policy references should come primarily from:

- 中华人民共和国教育部
- 上海市教育委员会

Community summaries never override official sources or teacher-provided school requirements.
